About

Full-scholarship Software Engineering student at Eastern Mediterranean University, focusing on game development with C, C# and Unity. Outside of coursework, I handle corporate communications and event logistics as a Board Member at EMU IE Club — working directly with companies, managing the Mentor-Mentee program, and helping organize large-scale events from start to finish.

A lot of what I know about working in a team came from doing it under pressure. Two years as a licensed badminton player taught me how to stay sharp and think fast when things aren't going your way. Game Jams pushed that further — shipping a working game with a small team in 48 hours doesn't leave room for misalignment. Programs like the Istanbul Education Summit rounded it out on the professional side. Across all of it, I've learned that teams work when everyone is clear on what they own and honest about what's blocking them.
